FROM THE JOURNAL

Where thoughts become poetry.

Poetry has always been one of the ways I make sense of the world around me.

I write about faith, identity, love, loneliness, womanhood, growth and the quiet conversations we have with ourselves.

Some pieces are soft. Some are uncomfortable. Some are questions. Some are prayers.

FEATURED PIECE

01

A LETTER TO MYSELF

Becoming

I am learning
that becoming
is not a straight line.

Some days
I arrive at myself
with certainty.

Other days,
I am still
finding the door.

And perhaps
that is the beauty
of becoming.

— Fatima Jalo
