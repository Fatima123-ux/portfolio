MY STORY

A Curious Mind,
A Creative Soul.

My journey has never followed a single straight line, but it has always revolved around my life razor — the one rule I live by, shaped by an excercise in Sahil Bloom's 5 Types Of Wealth:
"I’ll try everything, but I’ll only continue what brings me joy."
I chose this because I want to be a real-life Dora the Explorer. To collect different experiences and choose the best ones. To meet different people, and love the ones who melt my soul. To taste different dishes, and find the ones that chase my blues away. To do different things — not just to figure out what works but to choose what brings me joy. To live this way, I had to stay open-minded with a dead cat’s curiosity and pay attention to what I keep coming back to. I realized this single statement worked for me because When I hold on to what brings me joy, I do my best work for I am passionate about it. It doesn’t burn me out. It doesn’t feel like work, rather it gives me the clarity to create healthy boundaries in every part of my life. In the quest to try everything, i became a universe where different worlds collide.

"I’ll try everything, but I’ll only continue what brings me joy."

— Fatima Jalo
